# Getting there

!!! warning "No public access"
    **Fenswood Farm is not publicly-accessible**, these instructions are for research collaborators and students.
    
    Flying sessions are weather-dependent, so check for confirmation the day before and on the day.

## Location

Fenswood Farm is in the village of Long Ashton, a short journey from central Bristol.

**Address: Fenswood Farm, Wild Country Way, Long Ashton BS41 9AG.**

[Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1Fm3MfhieBKfhpuk8) / [What3Words](https://w3w.co/appeal.voted.unrealistic)

## Transport

Options from the University's Clifton campus:

### Bus (recommended)

Taking the bus is the most cost-effective and sustainable travel method.

The bus departs from Anchor Road, approximately 15-20 minutes’ walk from Queen’s Building, and takes around 15 minutes, followed by an additional 5-minute walk down Wild Country Lane to Fenswood Farm.

Routes (timetables linked):

* [X9 towards Nailsea](https://journeyplanner.travelwest.info/service/39-X9-%25-y10-1/direction/inbound)
* [X7 towards Clevedon](https://journeyplanner.travelwest.info/service/39-X7-_-y10-%25/direction/inbound)

Get off the bus at Warren Lane, just as the bus leaves the main Long Ashton village.

Both services are frequent, and timetables can be checked via the Travelwest or First Bus apps for the most accurate, real-time information. 

You may pay by contactless on the bus, but tickets are cheaper in advance via the First Bus app.

### Bicycle
Cycling is also a good option, and [National Cycle Route 33](https://www.walkwheelcycletrust.org.uk/find-a-route-on-the-national-cycle-network/route-33/) takes you from the city centre right past Fenswood Farm. There are no covered cycle storage facilities, but there are options for locking to.

### Taxi

Taxis average around £15–£20 each way from the Clifton campus. Local taxis are bookable via the Veezu app, Uber and other apps also operate in Bristol.

### Driving
Driving is also possible for those with access to a vehicle. Limited parking is available at the farm, so please plan accordingly and consider car sharing where possible.

## Access

Access is UCard controlled, and staff only - students should wait by the door and contact staff via Teams. The Lab entrance (and parking) is on the south side of the building - turn right through the main gate, along the building and round to the left.
